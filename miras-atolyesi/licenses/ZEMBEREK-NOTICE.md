The original dictionary is modified here into a deduplicated word/root list: entries and common verb stems are extracted, consonant softening and marked vowel-drop forms are added, and punctuation/metadata are omitted. A small set of game vocabulary is added. The project does not include or claim the full Zemberek morphological analyzer. Offline suffix checks in text-guard.js are approximate and do not determine sentence meaning.
